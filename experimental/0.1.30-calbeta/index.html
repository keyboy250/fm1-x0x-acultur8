Scope and limitations

Local native/WASM MIDI ingress and replay suites pass. Bounded physical USB mode-filtering/readback and selected notes/transport checks passed. Advanced remote generation, variation, Freeze/Commit and copy hardware replay remains open. Native-panel checks are separate.This is an experimental release, not full hardware qualification.

Reason external-clock clip/grid stability is unresolved. The 0.1.31 averaging and fine-tempo controls are excluded; no Reason-sync fix is claimed. The existing browser emulator retains its separately published build.

The inherited per-unit calibration facility is experimental and was measured on one unit. Other units require their own measurements.

Install carefully

Back up before updating. Retain exact previous firmware and its matching project, plus samples, unit calibration and the separate saved-default bank. Read installation and rollback instructions. The command-line installer is included in the complete bundle.
