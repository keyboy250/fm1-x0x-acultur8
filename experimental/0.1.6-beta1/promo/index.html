ON THE FM-1

Turn a pattern into
an arrangement.

01

A bridge of its own.

Duplicate a block as a variation and edit its copied notes independently. Build a fill or a bridge while keeping your original pattern.

02

Let a section breathe.

Set each block to 1–32 musical bars. Queue a block for the next bar, or let the arrangement move through its rows and wrap.

03

Change the shape. Undo.

Delete a block without deleting its underlying patterns, sounds or motion. Structural Undo/Redo and a visible SONG UNDO action help you retrace an edit.

04

Transpose with a home key.

Scale adherence brings transposed 303 notes into the shared key and scale. Use the harmony controls to keep bass variations connected.

Block variations copy pattern data. Sound, strip and FX settings remain shared across the project. The current lanes still store up to 64 steps; longer blocks repeat those patterns. Structural copy/delete/Undo actions require stopped playback.

THE EXTENDED FOUNDATION

Still a whole groovebox.

909 and 808 drums, two 303 bass voices, a break player, motion recording and effects — built on Charles Vestal's X0X and its component authors.

Extended Edition retains that foundation and adds 25 per-sound filter/limiter strips, explicitly applied reusable defaults, expanded lane timing, arrangement harmony and MIDI accompaniment/routing. The 909 strip batching improvement from 0.1.5 is included.

PLAY, TEST, FEEDBACK

Ready for an experiment?

This is a prerelease. Bounded hardware arrangement, save/restart, playback and one-note scale/MIDI checks passed. A 32-bar block took about 64 seconds at 121 BPM. The browser snare workflow was accepted as a useful starting point.

Broader heavy-load, external MIDI timing and overnight testing remain open. The separate autosave fix is not included. Keep a pre-update project backup and sample WAVs separately; older firmware can reject the newer arrangement format.
