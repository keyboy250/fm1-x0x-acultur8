Tested, with clear scope

The installed build passed a bounded control, listening and save/restart test on one unit: straight kick, triplet snare, independent settings retained after restart. Local qualification includes 134 required checks, 39 host scenarios, actual WASM, updater simulation and a byte-identical frozen-source rebuild.

Advanced remote CC action hardware acceptance and worst-case physical performance remain open. Reason external-clock stability is unresolved. The 0.1.31 averaging experiment and future alternate synth/drum engines are excluded. The public emulator remains on its separately published build.
