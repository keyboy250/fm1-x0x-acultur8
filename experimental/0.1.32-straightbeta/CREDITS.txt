PROJECT ORIGIN AND CREDITS

Acultur8 X0X Extended Edition builds on X0X for the M-VAVE FM-1,
created and maintained by Charles Vestal and upstream contributors.
Original project and source: https://github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-x0x
Original manual: https://charlesvestal.github.io/fm1-x0x/manual/
Original releases: https://github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-x0x/releases

X0X is itself a fork of Felucca by Leo Kuroshita (Hugelton Instruments).
Foundation project: https://github.com/hugelton/Felucca
Acultur8 adds the Extended Edition changes described in our release notes;
the original instrument, platform and upstream work retain their authorship.
This variant does not imply endorsement by the upstream authors or M-VAVE.

Additional origins retained from upstream:
- 9W9 / 8W8: athousanddetails; ER-99 and909 samples: Matthew Cieplak.
- Open303: Robin Schmidt; schwung-303 and schwung-tb3po: Charles Vestal;
  Devilfish extensions after jc303 (midilab), drive after dm-Rat (Dave Mollen);
  TB-3PO after Phazerville Hemisphere Suite by djphazer and contributors.
- BB Gen: mestela's schwung-breakbeat, used upstream with permission.
- Second-core research: Melodee by keremimo and Jangada by zednaked.
  https://github.com/keremimo/melodee
  https://github.com/zednaked/jangada
- Browser-emulator idea: Groove OS by Peter Gombos (credit, not imported code).
- Barlow and Terminus fonts, Fukiai web icons, and other retained notices:
  see the complete upstream LICENSING.md and accompanying license texts.
