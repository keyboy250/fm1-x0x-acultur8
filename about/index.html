ACULTUR8 / ABOUT

Music and experiments.
Made to play.

Acultur8 X0X is an experimental browser instrument and firmware variant for the M-VAVE FM-1, built on Charles Vestal's X0X and Felucca by Hügelton Instruments.

Visit Acultur8.comAcultur8 on Traxsource ↗

Hear, edit and test

The browser runs the shared C engine through WebAssembly. It lets you experiment with drum and bass patterns, import WAV slice banks with exact timing markers, and hear slices follow the sequencer tempo while keeping their pitch.

Development and credits

Experimental device firmware 0.1.9-query adds full-pattern and individual-part copy, preserved shared accents/swing, Undo and read-only RAM inspection, with bounded physical checks. The browser emulator now applies per-slice sound settings from Slice Review projects. Its firmware engine remains the earlier browser build; this browser update does not add native FM1 slice-settings upload.
