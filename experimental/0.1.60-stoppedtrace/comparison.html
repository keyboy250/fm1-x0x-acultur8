Hybrid layers, separate envelopes, velocity expression and 16 native preset slots.

INHERITED FOUNDATION

The wider engine.

909, 808, two 303 parts, BREAK, all 25 sound strips and the established song workflow.

QUALIFICATION OPEN

Know the bounds.

Physical core1 stack/deadline coverage and the cause of earlier .59 counter events remain open.
