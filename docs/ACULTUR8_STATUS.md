| --- | --- | --- |

| External MIDI pad | Planned | Browser events and real receiver checks separate |
| Internal pad and hardware slice upload | Later experiments | Target resources and device acceptance required |

The local roadmap tracks detailed milestones and acceptance. A browser alpha contains no promoted Acultur8 hardware installer. Passing host/WASM tests cannot replace physical CPU, MIDI, audio, storage and recovery measurements.
