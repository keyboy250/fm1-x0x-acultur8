WHERE THIS STARTED

When a rate sweep became a train wreck

The idea came from automating a rate parameter on a MIDI object. A smooth automation movement stepped through straight values, dotted divisions and triplets. The options were all useful, but moving between them made the rhythm feel clunky. The groove could turn into a kind of train wreck.

STRAIGHT mode grew out of that experience: keep the automation sweep on a deliberate list of straight values, then use a sound’s local override when you want triplets as a musical choice. A straight kick and a triplet snare can share the same composition.

Thirteen deliberate stops

Choose global STRAIGHT and sweep the rate knob without passing through dotted or triplet divisions.

A different groove for one sound

Select the snare. In EDIT → STRIP SYNC, choose STR+TRIPLET. Set its sequence rate to 1/16T while the kick stays at 1/16. All parts share one BPM; their step rates can differ.

Keep the useful details

Project save/reload, Undo/Redo and a searchable 753-entry MIDI reference. The new global/local mode controls are panel settings; existing MIDI addresses stay documented with their exact scope.
